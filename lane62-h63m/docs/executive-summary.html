What to expect

The calibrated instrument puts this file in the band 0.294 – 0.539 (mid 0.416) against a group best of 0.2778 and a top-5 cut-off of 0.3333. That is a MODEL number computed from a proxy measurement and a transfer constant — it is not a score and the organizer's number can land anywhere. Negative outcomes are deliverables: if it lands below 0.2778, the transfer constant was detector-specific and the calibration table on the leaderboard page is the thing to fix next.
