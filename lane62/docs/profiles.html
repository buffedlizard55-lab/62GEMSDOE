Deliverable 1 — shoreline elevation-versus-distance profiles with the flagged steps

The lane's first deliverable, as requested: the datum residual along the mapped Lahontan shoreline, with every flagged step and lateral deflection marked.

How the traces were made

  1. Isobase. robust (Huber, c=1.5) quadratic fit to the median highstand-band elevation of every 20 x 20 km block containing >= 300 pick pixels -- a rank-sufficient design, unlike a pixel-level fit confined to a narrow band. Result: RMS 3.57 m over 86 blocks; median residual 0.30 m, p16–p84 -10.24–10.71 m.
  2. Datum residual ζ = z − isobase, and its zero contour traced with marching squares on the 3730×3292 grid.
  3. Flags. Along each ordered trace, a vertical step is a change of ≥ 2.5 m between the mean of the 6 samples before and after a position; a lateral deflection is a ≥ 25.0° change of heading between the preceding and following chords. De-duplicated at 5 px.

Result: 44 traces, 3552 flags (800 vertical steps, 2752 lateral deflections).

Caveats a reader must carry

  • The isobase fit is degenerate in its linear part. Tilt is 0.0040 m/km east and 0.0038 m/km north — near zero, because the estimator is the median of a narrow elevation band per block, and a quadratic absorbs the literature's ~20 m northward tilt across the basin into its curvature terms. The fit is therefore adequate for locating the datum (RMS 3.57 m) but the tilt coefficients must not be read as a rebound measurement. That is flagged as IR-62-03.
  • The traces are the ζ = 0 contour of a block-median surface, not a digitised shoreline. At 100 m posting a 1–2 pixel shoreline cannot be picked directly; this is the proxy the lane said it would use and it is the reason the lane's dot class is heterogeneous (median credit 0).
  • the organiser's own training_features.tif was NOT obtainable: the DrivenData data page is login-walled (verified 2026-10-11: redirects to /accounts/login/).
