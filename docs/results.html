The holdout is pointed the wrong way (IR-62-HOLDOUT-01)

Spearman(HOLDOUT-DTI, ORGANIZER-CONFIRMED score) = -0.9141 across 10 artifacts scored on one identical frozen holdout (p=0.0002). Kendall τ -0.8140 (p=0.0015). Controlling for dot count the anti-correlation survives: partial Spearman -0.6810 (p=0.0302), and — of 8 count-matched pairs put the higher organizer score on the lower-holdout file.

Mechanism. The holdout withholds whole segments of the public fault catalogue and scores recovery of them. The organizer's Initial Prize Round scores a private set of faults labelled before the competition — not the public catalogue. So the holdout rewards dots that hug the catalogue and the organizer rewards dots that avoid it. The 0.2778 file was built by deleting 6,436 dots within 3 px of the catalogue: that raised its organizer score 0.2600 → 0.2778 and collapses its holdout DTI 0.1322 → 0.0053. The two objectives are in direct opposition.

Consequence for the promotion decision. The standing instruction is not to spend a weekly slot on an idea that has not beaten the current holdout best (0.1322, dotted-h19-5-d2-8-20261002-e56ea318af89-nan). Our artifact cannot beat that, and no valid instrument exists to overrule it — so the run card's verdict is negative and the file is marked NOT VALIDATED for submission. The negative result is the deliverable. Caveat, stated plainly: a frozen artifact is information-advantaged (built with the full catalogue in hand) whereas our arms see only the per-fold visible catalogue; for registry files that bias runs against them, since they were built to sit off the catalogue.
