Results — 2026-10-12 session (H62-B)

Every number below is MEASURED in this checkout (the script that produced it is named), OWNER-REPORTED (a score the owner quoted; there is no organizer receipt for any file in this repository), OFFICIAL (quoted from the competition pages) or MODEL (algebra on the published metric). A MODEL number is never written as a score.

1. The question the brief asked: why did h33-2-b2 score 0.2778?

Three separate things, and only the third is a property of the geology.

(a) The metric is recall-dominated and the marginal bar is low. DTI = TP_w / (TP_w + 0.2·FP_w + 0.8·FN_w) with k(d) = max(1 − d/300 m, 0) [OFFICIAL, page 967]. Adding one unit dot that realises kernel weight k raises the numerator by k and the denominator by exactly 0.2, so a dot pays for itself whenever k > 0.2·DTI. At 0.2778 that bar is 0.0556 — any dot with better than a 5.6 % chance of landing within 300 m of a hidden new fault raises the score [MODEL].

(b) Half the variance of the group's scores is structure, not geology. Regressing the owner-reported scores of the 87 distinct registry rasters on ten structural measurements — no geology at all — gives leave-one-out R² = 0.586, Spearman 0.781 [MEASURED, research/registry_measurements.json]. The signs are exactly what the algebra predicts:

propertySpearman vs score

mean self-kernel (dot-to-dot redundancy)−0.55

fraction of dots sitting on the catalogue−0.49
90th percentile of distance-to-catalogue+0.46

So: isolated single pixels, off the catalogue, spread over the whole grid, and not too many of them. The top nine distinct score levels all have mean self-kernel 0.016–0.018; every raster with self-kernel ≥ 0.23 scores ≤ 0.2477.

(c) Within that structure, placement still matters — by about 1.1× for the champion. Against an independent truth set (below), the champion's dots earn 0.1324 credit per dot when a hard-core random field of the same mass earns 0.1190: an 11 % edge. That edge is what this session set out to widen.

2. Building an instrument that is actually calibrated

The holdout the protocol prescribes was measured to be anti-correlated with the organizer's score (IR-62-HOLDOUT-01: Spearman −0.91 over 10 confirmed artifacts). This session found the mechanism and fixed it.

IR-62-HOLDOUT-02 — the catalogue holdout is unsatisfiable by arithmetic, not by lack of skill. The kernel reaches 300 m. Every competitive emission deletes every dot within 300 m of the mapped catalogue, because the organizer does not score mapped faults and the group has an organizer-confirmed +0.0178 for deleting them (h33: 0 % within 200 m; this session's file: 0 % within 300 m, minimum 400 m). A dot set with no dot within 300 m of any catalogue pixel therefore has TP_w = 0 against withheld catalogue pixels necessarily. Measured: pooled DTI = 0.000000 for this session's artifact. The better an artifact is at being off-catalogue, the more certainly it scores zero. Fixed once, in the template: gems62.holdout.evaluate now takes a truth argument so the withheld population can be an independent fault set.

The instrument that is validated. Truth = the USGS State Geologic Map Compilation (1:750 k) rasterised on the competition grid, minus the competition catalogue (79,615 px; only 4.8 % of it falls on catalogue pixels, so it is not a re-digitisation of the same lines). Scoring domain = footprint minus catalogue ± 3 px. Quantity = mean 300 m kernel credit per dot, i.e. TP_w / M.

Bars, all MEASURED on the same truth and the same mass as the artifact: hard-core Poisson control 0.1190 (an independent 40,000-dot draw in Experiment 2 gave 0.1197, so the bar is stable to ~0.6 %); uniform random control 0.1075; champion h33 0.1324; this lane's previous artifact H62-A 0.1347.

Two readings, and both are stated because they cannot be separated here. Either off-catalogue faults really do live on steep, high, thin-cover ground and not on the pluvial lake plains — or the SGMC compilation is a bedrock map that simply does not map basin-floor faults, and the instrument is blind to the population this lane exists to find. The four-times-repeated measurement that the pluvial band is below chance (previous sessions: 0.03× and 0.61×; this session, with a completely different band construction and a different base-level estimator: 0.026× on the band and 0.26× for its own habitat null) is consistent with both.

This is the honest limit of the whole exercise, and it is why the lane's verdicts have been negative: the lane's target population is exactly the population no local truth set contains.

The mean credit is non-monotone in the first few thousand dots (it rises from 0.2037 to 0.2190 before falling), which is a real property of the ranking rather than a transcription error: the top-ranked candidates are the traces with the largest single cross-strike relief, and those are not the traces that pass through the most fault-rich ground. It costs nothing — the projection is maximised at 37,654 either way.

37,654 is chosen: the projection peaks there, and it is also the mass of the group's organizer-confirmed best.

The projection is stable across the entire plausible truth-mass range (0.353 at N = 6,000, 10,000 and 13,704) because the champion's implied credit and the denominator both scale with N — which is why it is reported as a ratio to the champion rather than an absolute. A second, simpler projection used in the mass sweep gives 0.3484 at the shipped mass; the 0.005 spread between the two is IR-62-PROJ-01 and is 24× smaller than the ±0.12 residual of the calibration regression that shrinks them both.

6. Calibrating the projection against reality

Applying the identical projection to the 12 other registry artifacts whose scores are known, and regressing actual on projected: slope 0.325, intercept 0.175, r = 0.826, p = 0.0005 (through-origin slope 1.076). So the projection is a real predictor but heavily compressed, and the shrunk expectation for this file is

0.175 + 0.325 × 0.353 = 0.290

Residuals are large (±0.12). The worst case is p49: credit 0.1759 (1.33× the champion, higher than this file's 1.27×), projection 0.359, actual 0.2376 — an over-prediction of 0.121. The eight isolated-dot artifacts other than p49 are all under-predicted, by 0.004 to 0.074.
