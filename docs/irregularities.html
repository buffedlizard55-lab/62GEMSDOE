IR-62-01HighOpen — no organizer scoreNo submission from this repository has been scored. Every number is HOLDOUT-DTI, MEASURED or MODEL. The public leaderboard was read 2026-10-11 (#1 = 0.4133).Never write a projection as a score; label the verdict negative and let the selector decide.
IR-62-02MediumVerified — the portal range error has two root causes`training_features.tif` declares nodata = -3.4028234663852886e+38 (M1); a submission carrying a nodata tag of NaN can be read as out of range by a whole-raster check (M2).This repository writes all-finite with nodata = None and refuses to emit a receipt if any of the 12,279,160 cells is non-finite or outside [0, 1].
IR-62-03HighOpen — the isobase fit is degenerate in its linear partFitted tilt is 0.0040 m/km east and 0.0038 m/km north, against the literature's ~20 m northward tilt across the basin. The estimator (median of a narrow elevation band per 20×20 km block) has a compressed dynamic range, and a quadratic can absorb the tilt into curvature.The fit is used only to locate the datum (RMS 3.57 m). Its tilt coefficients are NOT reported as a rebound measurement anywhere on this site.
IR-62-04HighVerified — the strict uniqueness leg cannot be satisfied by any fileThe top comparator, 17GEMSDOE_E-proba-multiscale_20260930T044527Z.tif, has 5,167,373 positive pixels — 100 % of the footprint — so every prediction scores 1.0 of one-sided overlap against it. A further 8 comparators are dense enough that their 3 px dilation also covers the map.The leg is reported under both readings: strict (all 508 comparators) and informative (the 321 whose 3 px dilation covers ≤ 50 % of the footprint). The verdict uses the informative leg plus rank correlation, and the strict value is printed so a reviewer can see it is degenerate rather than hidden.
IR-62-05MediumOpen — the selector was uninformative about the budgetMean DTI on the mass-matched instrument B′ rose monotonically to the largest budget tested (86968 dots); there is no interior optimum inside the lane's product form.The budget was therefore bounded by the lane's own product form and by the uniqueness gate, not by the selector. Stated on the Results page rather than smoothed over.
IR-62-06MediumOpen — the lidar band order is owner-documented, not organizer-tagged`lidar_scarp_features_u8.tif` carries no band tags, so the mapping of band 8 to cross-scarp and band 7 to upface is taken from the group's own written documentation. This is the class of defect the group logged as IR-70-DATA-01.The evidence field addresses the tagged topo stack by tag and the untagged lidar stack by documented order, and the provenance is printed on this page and in evidence/evidence_field.json.
IR-62-07CriticalOpen — competition rasters are not organizer-authenticated herelabels.tif, existing_faults.tif and sample_submission.tif are owner-mirrored files with hashes matching the group's pinned manifest, but the DrivenData data page is login-walled, so their authenticity was not established from this sandbox.Do not treat the mirrors as official on this repository's word alone; re-download through an authorized session and compare sha256 before relying on any final-round decision.

IR-62-09LowFixed — bootstrap interval did not contain its own point estimateAn earlier draft of src/gems62/holdout.py computed the confidence interval with the algebra T/(0.2T+0.2F+0.8n) while the point estimate came from dti_from_components; the two agreed only to the third decimal place.The bootstrap now recomputes the plug-in value and raises AssertionError unless it equals the pooled DTI to 1e-9. Covered by tests/test_holdout.py.
IR-62-10LowFixed — rank-correlation leg sampled the wrong populationThe gate first sampled this file's own positive pixels for the correlation leg, which makes one side constant and the coefficient undefined.The sample is now drawn from the valid footprint, so the leg measures the correlation of two value fields as intended.

What this repository cannot do from this sandbox (measured 2026-10-11)

  • TLS to drivendata.org, raw.githubusercontent.com, sciencebase.gov, earthquake.usgs.gov and the USGS 3DEP endpoints is unreachable from the shell; the DrivenData pages were read through the browsing path, which is why citations here carry a read date.
  • No GPU and ~2 vCPU, so no neural detector was trained; this lane is deliberately physics-first.
  • The organizer's own training_features.tif (19 bands) is unavailable, so the lane works from the DEM plus the group's derived stacks. Every claim that would need an organizer band is labelled as such or omitted.
