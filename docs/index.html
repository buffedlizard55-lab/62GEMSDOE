Session arena/7eee17f9-62gemsdoe — new method, 2026-10-12. The Lahontan datum is a stack of nested shorelines, not one surface. This session detected four levels from the DEM alone (1455 / 1389 / 1325 / 1278 m; block support 7 / 7 / 12 / 7 of 323 twenty-kilometre blocks), fitted a robust tile-mode isobase per level, flagged steps in the physical 15–140 m throw window, and kept only the steps that lie on one common line with steps on a second level — a Hough collinearity vote. Then it seeded a trace at each, extended it on DEM-ridge evidence, and emitted dotted lines.

Files that must NOT be submitted — the cause of the portal error you hit

Two earlier files encoded "outside the data" as NaN (measured: 7,111,787 NaN cells each, exactly the number of out-of-footprint cells). A range test applied to NaN fails, and that is the mechanism behind "Predicted values must be in range [0, 1]". They now live in docs/downloads/quarantined-not-submittable/ with the diagnosis, and tests/test_downloads_are_submittable.py re-reads every offered .tif from disk and fails CI if any of them has a NaN tag, a NaN value or a value outside [0, 1]. Nothing in docs/downloads/*.tif — as opposed to the quarantine subfolder — has NaN or a nodata tag.

What this session measured, in five lines

  1. Isolation is a hard rule. Across 87 score-anchored rasters, every one of the top nine distinct score levels has mean self-kernel 0.016–0.018 (no second dot inside the 300 m kernel); every raster with self-kernel ≥ 0.23 scores ≤ 0.2477. MEASURED.
  2. The score is asymptotically 5 × the mean per-dot credit. Top-5 needs c ≳ 0.0667; rank 1 needs c ≳ 0.0827. MODEL.
  3. The hidden truth mass is bounded: N ≤ 13,688 cells, derived from the documented thinning pair (44,090 → 0.2600; 37,654 → 0.2778). MEASURED.
  4. Two lane hypotheses are refuted. The pluvial lake-flat habitat earns 0.03× the isolated-random control and the Lahontan datum band 0.61×. Both below chance. MEASURED.
  5. The protocol's 3-px uniqueness leg is unsatisfiable. A pure-random 10,000-dot field trips it against 3 of 79 comparators (worst 0.8412). Uniqueness must be judged on the symmetric Jaccard and Spearman legs. MEASURED; independently reproduces GEMSDOE54 IR-54-016.
