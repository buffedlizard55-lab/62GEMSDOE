First Deliverable: Shoreline Elevation-versus-Distance Profiles

The Lake Lahontan highstand (~13–15 ka) forms a near-horizontal, isochronous datum across the northwestern Great Basin. After removing post-pluvial isostatic rebound (a ~22 m domal upwarp) and regional northward tilt using a robust 2D polynomial isobase, abrupt residual steps ($|jump| \ge z_{\text{min}} \sqrt{2} \sigma_{\text{pairs}} \approx 10.55\text{ m}$) and lateral azimuth kinks ($> 30^\circ$) identify active post-15 ka fault displacements.
