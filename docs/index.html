What the file is

  • flat-pixel stand filter (local tile mode, 8 km tiles) inside the basin-wide stand band 1157–1455 m
  • robust quadratic isobase (Huber IRLS) — absorbs post-pluvial rebound, cannot follow one scarp
  • Frangi ridge of |∇(z − isobase)|, offset measured at ±250 m
  • gates: evidence ≥ 0.10, step ≥ 2 m, ≤ 1500 m from a bench, step trace crossing the datum contours ≥ 30°
  • 2,003 loci flagged → 400 kept (2 km suppression) → 221 traces → 1,660 dots

The cross-cut gate is what separates a tectonic scarp (steps across its own trace, so it crosses contours) from a fan-head riser, wave-cut bluff or berm (runs parallel to contours). It is a defence, not a proof.
