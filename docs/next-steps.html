Limitations — stated as limitations, not as future work

  1. No local instrument can certify novelty or competitiveness. This session's own screening refuted a hypothesis that looked obvious (lake flats, 0.03× control) and confirmed one that is then unusable (LiDAR scarps, 2.2× control, duplicate-drift). Both outcomes came from a proxy.
  2. The datum lane is dominated, and now for a measured reason. The Lahontan datum band scores 0.61× the isolated-random control on the off-catalogue instrument. Either the band is not fault-rich, or a 1:750 k compilation cannot see 2 m datum offsets. The two cannot be separated without the organizer's truth.
  3. Coverage of the delivered file is one sub-region of one physical process. 10,000 dots on shoreline benches. The metric needs ~40,000 isolated dots to matter at all.
  4. The 25 m DEM mosaic is unusable basin-wide (IR-62-15): 55.74 % footprint coverage and an int16-decimetre ceiling at 3276.7 m that clips the eastern Sierra. The 100 m mirror was used instead (99.97 % coverage, sha256 matches the pin).
  5. The weekly slot decision is not made here. Per the brief, promotion is a separate selector step within the weekly cap.
  6. Sandbox reachability is a hard constraint. drivendata.org returns HTTP 000; the USGS hosts are unreachable. github.com, api.github.com and codeload.github.com work, which is how the registry and the external layers were obtained. Every competition-page citation in this repository is therefore either an owner-supplied link or a mirror with a read date.
  7. No file in this repository has ever been scored. organizer_score is null everywhere.

This ranking replaces the "what to try next" list above where they disagree; the measurements behind every line are in evidence/h62j* and docs/run-card.html.

  1. Make the selector decision about GEMSDOE32's never-submitted field. Unchanged from 2026-10-11 and still first: 45,000 dots at instrument-B credit 0.29294, 3.0× the submitted family's 0.09892, with no owner-reported score attached anywhere. Minutes of work, a lane-boundary decision, and the number must be discounted hard.
  2. Run the collar ladder — it is now the cheapest untried experiment in the group. The band table (this session, evidence/h62j-bands.json) measures per-dot credit 0.178 / 0.153 / 0.108 inside 500 m of a mapped trace against 0.018 beyond 4 km, with same-band controls giving ratios 1.33 / 1.21 / 1.29 versus 0.51. Every lane here emits dots pushed away from the catalogue by a 250–300 m collar. Emitting the same detector at collars {200, 300, 500, 1000} m and comparing model DTI costs one sweep per lane and settles whether "new geometry" should mean near the trace rather than far from it. Note the asymmetry that makes this non-trivial: dots within ~250 m of the catalogue are masked out of the organiser's truth, so the gain has to come from the flanking damage zone, which is exactly what 0.108–0.178 per dot says is there.
  3. H62-K, along-strike continuation of mapped systems into DEM-evident but unmapped ground. Still untried, still the best physical idea in the lane, and now with a measured reason to expect it to work: continuation is by construction 0–1,500 m off a trace, the band this session found pays 2–5× the far field. Inputs already in the checkout: qfault_attributes.csv, trace_segments_utm11.csv, gdr_qfaults_traces.csv.
  4. The datum lane needs coverage, not cleverness. 9.42 % of footprint cells are measurable by the doublet at all (IR-62-22), which caps mass at 10,870 dots and the lane's ceiling at 5 × credit = 0.203. Reaching a footprint-wide datum field means the remaining 12–16 3DEP tiles on the dem-fetch.yml runner (~90 min, >90 MB) — a real cost, worth spending only after (2) or (3) show the method beats chance at full coverage.
  5. Geothermal: stop using thermal occurrences as structure, for this metric. Measured 0.75–0.84× control for Hot/Warm cells and corridors, 0.10–0.21× for mapped vents (evidence/h62j2-geothermal.json, mass-matched controls). If the geothermal lane still wants thermal data, it must be as a prior weighted by the band table, not as a placement rule — and the burden is now on it to show that.
