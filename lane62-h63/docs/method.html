The metric, reduced to the one quantity that matters

With T = TP_w, F = FP_w, K = |G| (the hidden truth mass), α = 0.2, β = 0.8, and S the emitted mass:

DTI = T / ( α·(T + F) + β·K )  ≈  T / ( α·S + β·K )      (exact when emitted kernel mass = credit)
1/DTI = α + α·(F/T) + β·(K/T)

So at fixed K the whole ranking is credit per unit emitted mass, c = T/S. Two consequences drive every choice here:

  • Mass is not free but density along a true line is. Adding one unit of mass raises the denominator by exactly α wherever it lands, so an added cell pays for itself iff its kernel credit exceeds α·DTI ≈ 0.06. A cell sitting on a hidden fault pixel returns full credit and zero false-positive mass — so packing dots tightly along a corroborated lineament costs nothing, which is why the shipped file is not thinned to the 300 m kernel.
  • For random dots c depends only on the truth's area density, c_random = (π R²/3)·K/A with R = 3 cells and A the scored area. Measured against that closed form on this grid: 0.11254 predicted vs 0.09898 measured (SGMC off-catalogue), 0.11130 vs 0.10028 (catalogue). That agreement is what makes “lift over chance” a measured quantity rather than a slogan.

Why the channels are catalogue-free

Every channel is a transform of the pinned 100 m DEM or of the official 19-band stack. None of them reads the catalogue, so nothing can leak and the full catalogue and the full SGMC off-catalogue set can both be used as instruments. This is a direct consequence of the holdout result below.

Combination by rank average (corroboration)

Raw amplitudes are on incomparable scales with wildly non-Gaussian tails, so each channel is converted to an average-rank field in [0, 1] inside the valid footprint and the fields are then averaged. Averaging ranks, not amplitudes, is equivalent to requiring that several physically independent expressions agree on the same cell.

Emission

  1. Restrict to cells at least 300 m from the USGS/INGENIOUS catalogue. Those pixels are masked from scoring, so mass spent there is mass wasted. Measured: the SGMC faults the catalogue misses sit a median of 2319 m away and 79% of them are beyond 1 km.
  2. Non-maximum suppression in a 3×3 window (radius 1), which removes plateaus without pulling dots apart to the kernel distance.
  3. Keep the 90,000 highest-belief peaks.
  4. Write 1.0 on those cells, 0.0 elsewhere, 0.0 outside the footprint, no nodata tag.

The two remaining policy axes (suppression radius and mass) were swept on the calibrated instrument; the shipped point is the arg-max of the pessimistic projection, not of the optimistic one.
