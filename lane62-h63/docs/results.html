AUC 0.50 is chance. This is the measured explanation for a fact the group has been carrying for six weeks: its supervised branch tops out at 0.1563 while its heuristic lineament branch reaches 0.2778. The 0.2298 “labels DTI” the flagship repository reports is an in-domain number — the shipped model had been trained on every label.

Leakage canary: every feature was also scored alone on the held-out blocks. The highest single-feature AUC is 0.577 (dem_slope_deg / dem_gradmag), far below the 0.90 leakage tripwire — so the null above is not a bug in one channel, it is the absence of transferable signal.

Read the catalogue column too: it is anti-correlated with the score. The best submissions have essentially zero skill at finding catalogue faults (0.0100 for the champion) while the worst-but-one has the highest (0.2678). The hidden set is not where the catalogue is.

3. The one confound that could fake the result, measured

Every DEM channel is a form of local relief, and the USGS State Geologic Map Compilation draws most of its faults in ranges — so a detector that simply finds mountains would score well on the proxy instrument without finding a single fault. Measured:

The emission is mildly biased toward rough ground — 13.7% of its dots sit in the steepest decile against a 10 % base rate — but it is less rough than the proxy truth itself (821 vs 600). So the confound is real, quantified, and not large enough on its own to explain a 1.82× lift. Stated rather than dismissed.
